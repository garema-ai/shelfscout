04 / THE METHOD

A small, explainable agent.

01

Collect

Record store, exact pack, listed price, promotion and the source of the price. The included sample is made up.

02

Match

Compare brand, variant and pack size. A barcode is the next step for verified SKU matching.

03

Check

Set aside unclear, promotional or conflicting prices. Queue anything uncertain for human review.

04

Explain

Show a channel median and gap only when clean observations exist in both formats. Keep the store counts visible.
